PULSEGRID STUDIO

A space for your next idea.

Make some noise

Press Space to play. Launch individual clips with their triangle, or a full scene from the right-hand column. Launches wait for the selected quantization boundary.

Select a clip to edit. Double-click to add a note, drag to move, drag its right edge to resize. Right-click or Delete removes selected notes. B toggles drawing.

The computer keyboard A W S E D F T G Y H U J K plays C4–C5. Z/X changes octave. Web MIDI is available through the bottom MIDI button.

Shape and arrange

Tab switches Session and Arrangement. Double-click an empty arrangement lane to insert its selected clip. Drag or resize instances without changing the source. The Automation button edits a track-volume envelope.

Drop an audio file to create a sample clip. Start and end markers, reverse, gain, and tempo-synced repitch are non-destructive. No phase-vocoder time stretching is claimed.

On MIDI tracks, Record overdubs keyboard/MIDI notes. On audio tracks, it requests microphone access and captures raw PCM. Monitoring is off by default to prevent feedback.

Local-first · No account · No external audio assets · Save embeds samples · Export renders actual audio
